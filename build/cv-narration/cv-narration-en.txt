This is Santiago Llaguno's CV, read aloud.

Santiago describes himself as an AI architect focused on conversational and voice agents. He designs and deploys agentic and conversational AI solutions end to end: he learns the customer's process, builds the solution in a creative, measurable way that makes the most of resources, and measures the quality of each solution with tailored evaluations. He works on Claude, with its API, the Agent SDK and MCP, and on ElevenLabs Agents, in voice and in text. He's a Claude Certified Architect at the Professional level and studied applied mathematics in computer science. He brings analytical skill, firsthand sales experience and technical command to build agents, agentic solutions and software tailored to each business's needs and processes. In 2026 he founded Sonelo, which builds conversational AI role play platforms and commerce agents for real estate agencies, timeshare sales rooms, auto showrooms and jewelry stores.

Selected work.

A virtual sales room with voice, at Los Cabos Sotheby's International Realty. Santiago designed and built a virtual sales room for luxury properties and developments. He drew on his experience as an advisor to model the sales conversation and integrated voice agents with call, email and message automations, to create an immersive experience that increases client engagement, automates lead capture and improves retention.

A voice sales trainer, Sonelo Trainer. Santiago built a role play platform for sales floors. He designed customer archetypes from each company's processes and data, so reps practice realistically with AI, and an analysis of every session that gives visibility into the team's performance and skills. The platform speeds up training and onboarding and aims to raise average sales.

A voice shopping agent, adaptable to each business's catalog. Santiago designed it to sell like a good advisor: it recommends based on the customer's profile, checks live availability instead of guessing, and hands sensitive topics to a person. He built the first version on public data from a dive trip operator and designed seven blocks of tailored evaluations to validate it.

And a budget and indicators platform for one of the largest foundations in Los Cabos. Santiago designed and built a custom platform, with AI built in, that unifies its budget, income and expense files into a single reliable source. Its management and board reports went from hours to minutes. He built it custom tools to create visuals and checks that flag when a figure doesn't add up. He also designed and teaches its staff a course on AI applied to daily work, in eight sessions.

At build days: in San Francisco, with Anthropic, in September 2026, he built Praise Gap, which mines competitors' reviews and keeps only claims that survive adversarial verification. And in Mexico City, with Salesforce and Anthropic, in August 2026, a CRM briefing agent in Agentforce, with voice and cognitive load evals.

Experience.

Since 2026 he has been founder and Forward Deployed Engineer at Sonelo, where he designed and built two business lines: Sonelo Trainer, with which he makes training and onboarding easier for sales teams, and Sonelo Solutions, with which he automates call answering, appointment booking and reception. His method: he maps the conversations each agent will handle and where they get stuck, and from that map he designs its evaluations.

And from 2017 to 2020 he worked at Stratego Management and Right Source. At Stratego, as Workplace Climate Manager, he ran studies at plants across Mexico with groups of twenty to one hundred people. At Right Source, as Documentation and Process Analyst, he automated document formatting with Python and Office macros and cut a week of work to days.
