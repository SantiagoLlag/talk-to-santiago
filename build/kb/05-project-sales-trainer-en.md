Scope: The voice role-play sales trainer that Santiago Llaguno built for sales teams, and the demo he built for a car dealership network with incomplete information, described at a technical level.

## What is the sales trainer? Voice role play for sales teams
Santiago Llaguno built a voice sales trainer based on role play. Salespeople talk with the sales trainer to practice real sales situations out loud. A reduced version of the sales trainer exists as a demo.
Source: ledger P4

## Which industries is the sales trainer for? Retail, real estate and automotive
Santiago Llaguno built the sales trainer for sales teams in retail, real estate and the automotive industry.
Source: ledger P4

## Is there a fourth industry for the sales trainer? Timeshare, in testing
In September 2026, a fourth version of Santiago Llaguno's sales trainer, for timeshare sales at Los Cabos hotels, is in testing before launch. The timeshare version has not launched yet.

Source: ledger P4b

## What do salespeople practice with the sales trainer? Product, objections, closing and financing
With the sales trainer that Santiago Llaguno built, salespeople practice product knowledge, handling objections, closing the sale and explaining financing options.
Source: ledger P4

## How does the sales trainer work in the automotive industry? Lead qualification
In the automotive version of the sales trainer, Santiago Llaguno added lead qualification in both text and voice. The automotive lead qualification is based on real sales conversations. The goal in the automotive industry is to speed up onboarding for sales teams with high turnover.
Source: ledger P4

## How does Santiago decide with incomplete information? The car dealership demo
Santiago Llaguno once built a demo for a network of car dealerships without knowing the network's internal processes. Santiago had only the general need, which was lead handling for a large sales team, plus public research on the dealership network.
Source: ledger P5

## How did Santiago structure the car dealership demo? Two parts
Santiago Llaguno split the car dealership demo into two parts. The first part of the car dealership demo handles lead qualification. The second part provides agents for the sales floor, with role plays on lead qualification, car models and financing.
Source: ledger P5

## How did the car dealership demo go? Well received, proposal under review
The car dealership network liked the demo that Santiago Llaguno built. As of September 2026, Santiago's proposal to the car dealership network is under review.
Source: ledger P5

## What would Santiago do differently on the car dealership demo? Ask for real conversations first
Looking back on the car dealership demo, Santiago Llaguno would ask for real sales conversations earlier, under an NDA. With real conversations from the start, Santiago could build a role play that is closer to how the dealership's salespeople actually sell.
Source: ledger P5
