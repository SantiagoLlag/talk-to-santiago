## What poem did Santiago set to music with ElevenLabs? Ode to the Hammer
Santiago Llaguno writes a lot of poetry. "La oda al martillo" (Ode to the Hammer) is an epic poem he wrote on a plane, when he wanted to write about an object instead of something personal. He chose the hammer as a symbol and as an object: forging weapons and shackles, Vulcan, Thor, and the flags that carry a hammer.

Source: ledger G5, G5b

## How did Santiago set Ode to the Hammer to music? An epic style in ElevenLabs
Santiago Llaguno set "la oda al martillo" to music with ElevenLabs when the platform had just launched; it was one of the first things he made on ElevenLabs. The song runs about two and a half minutes and is sung in Spanish. Santiago asked for an epic style: a male lead voice, martial percussion, brass and anvil rhythms.

Source: ledger G5, G5b

## What does Ode to the Hammer say? The original Spanish text
Santiago Llaguno's poem "la oda al martillo" has four stanzas of four lines each, written in Spanish:

Flama danzante, al son del martillo
Con tinta de sangre se escribe el destino
Se forman grilletes del negro y del indio
Se fraguan hazañas del gallardo caudillo

Espadas danzantes que pierden su brillo
De padres y hermanos se prenden los lirios
Letal fiero cobre de babilonios y asirios
El son de las armas, una oda al martillo

Tuyo es El Hierro que sostiene al castillo
Señor de la fragua, poderoso Vulcano
Defensor es el fuego que sostiene tu mano
El amor se sostiene a través de tu anillo

La tierra se labra con su raudo rastrillo
Tu imagen honrada en banderas sagradas
De hombres caídos defendiendo sus patrias
Cuando de Thor los cielos atruena el martillo

Source: ledger G5b
